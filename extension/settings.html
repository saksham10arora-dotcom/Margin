Subscriptions

A plan you already pay for, used through the company's own command-line tool you are signed in to. Each counts against that plan, so Margin writes at most 30 notes a day with each.

API keys

Saved on this Mac in ~/.margin/keys.env, readable by you only. Margin never shows a key again once saved, and never sends one anywhere but its own provider. Keys you keep in your environment or in ~/.config/keys.env are used too.
